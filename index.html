About Me

I’m a Research Data Analyst at the MODEL-AD Lab, Stark Neurosciences Research Institute, Indiana University School of Medicine. My work bridges clinical and pre-clinical research, focusing on Alzheimer’s disease data analysis, automation, and imaging pipelines using Imaris XT and Python.

With a background in dentistry and a master’s in Health Informatics, I combine analytical rigor with a clinical understanding to build reproducible workflows that advance neuroscience research.
